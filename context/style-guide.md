# Style guide

Mechanics for every language. Voice is in [brand-voice.md](brand-voice.md); structure and SEO rules are in [../docs/article-spec.md](../docs/article-spec.md); humanizing rules are in [../docs/humanizing.md](../docs/humanizing.md).

- No em dashes. Use a comma, colon or full stop.
- Sentence case headings.
- Paragraphs of 2 to 4 sentences.
- Numbers as digits; dates as DD/MM/YYYY; currency in rupees.
- Planet names: English first, Sanskrit in brackets on first use (Saturn (Shani)).
- Hinglish in Latin script, no italics, no glossary brackets.
- Each language follows its own Indic style guide section in humanizing.md.
